01 · MATERI

Grammar Pocket Guide

Enam topik grammar yang paling sering keluar di kelas dan ujian SMA. Ketuk kartu untuk membuka penjelasan singkatnya.

Simple Present & Past Tense +

Simple Present dipakai untuk kebiasaan, fakta umum, dan jadwal tetap. Simple Past dipakai untuk kejadian yang sudah selesai di masa lalu, biasanya dengan verb-2.

She studies every night. → She studied all night before the exam.
Present & Past Continuous +

Continuous tense (be + verb-ing) menunjukkan aksi yang sedang berlangsung, baik sekarang maupun pada satu titik waktu tertentu di masa lalu.

I am writing an essay right now. / They were watching a movie when I called.
Passive Voice +

Digunakan saat objek lebih penting daripada pelaku. Rumus: object + to be + verb-3 (+ by subject).

The report was written by the committee last week.
Conditional Sentences +

Type 1 untuk kemungkinan nyata, type 2 untuk pengandaian yang tidak nyata di masa sekarang, dan type 3 untuk penyesalan di masa lalu.

If it rains, I will stay home. / If I were you, I would apologize.
Reported Speech +

Saat menyampaikan ucapan orang lain, tense biasanya mundur satu tingkat dan kata ganti/keterangan waktu berubah menyesuaikan.

"I am tired," she said. → She said (that) she was tired.
Jenis Teks: Narrative, Recount, Descriptive, Report +

Narrative menceritakan kisah fiksi dengan konflik dan resolusi. Recount menceritakan pengalaman nyata secara kronologis. Descriptive menggambarkan ciri suatu objek. Report menjelaskan fakta umum tentang suatu kelompok atau fenomena.

Legenda "Malin Kundang" = narrative. "My Trip to Bali" = recount.

Reading Comprehension

Baca teks berikut lalu jawab pertanyaannya. Jawaban akan langsung ditandai benar atau salah.

Lake Toba: A Natural Wonder

Lake Toba is the largest volcanic lake in the world, located in North Sumatra, Indonesia. It was formed by a massive volcanic eruption thousands of years ago. In the middle of the lake lies Samosir Island, which is almost as big as the country of Singapore.

The lake is surrounded by green hills and small villages where the Batak people live. Tourists often visit Lake Toba to enjoy the cool weather, try traditional Batak food, and learn about local culture. Many visitors say that the view of the lake at sunrise is truly unforgettable.

1. What is Lake Toba known as?

2. What is located in the middle of the lake?

3. Who traditionally lives around Lake Toba?
